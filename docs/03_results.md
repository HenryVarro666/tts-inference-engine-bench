## 3.1 LLM-backbone: vLLM vs TensorRT-LLM vs eager

Same exported `Qwen2ForCausalLM` backbone (1.543B, bf16), prefill 64 + 48 autoregressive tokens, batch 1, single H100 NVL.

| Engine | total (s) | per-token (ms) | tokens/s | vs eager (throughput) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| eager PyTorch (StaticCache) | 3.698 | 46.71 | 21.4 | 1.0× |
| TensorRT-LLM 1.3.0 (PyTorch backend) | 0.226 | 4.71 | 212.5 | 9.9× |
| **vLLM 0.17.1** | **0.106** | **2.21** | **452.8** | **21.2×** |

Raw per-run seconds:

| Engine | run1 | run2 | run3 | best |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| eager | — (single) | — | — | 3.698 |
| TensorRT-LLM | 0.2317 | 0.2259 | 0.2300 | 0.2259 |
| vLLM | 0.1075 | 0.1060 | 0.1061 | 0.1060 |

> Eager breakdown: prefill 1.456 s + decode 2.242 s (46.7 ms/token). vLLM and TRT-LLM both do the *whole* prefill+decode in well under the eager decode alone.

## 3.2 End-to-end dots-tts latency split (eager integration)

One 7.68 s utterance synthesized with the full dots-tts pipeline (built-in per-stage profiler, bf16, 1×H100, `num_steps=10` ODE):

| Stage | seconds | share | real-time factor (RTF) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Flow-matching **DiT (diffusion/ODE)** | 18.56 | **67.8%** | 2.42 |
| **LLM backbone (Qwen2 AR)** | 5.69 | **20.8%** | 0.74 |
| patch_encoder | 1.86 | 6.8% | 0.24 |
| latent_decoder | 0.45 | 1.6% | 0.06 |
| speaker/vocoder/prefill/misc | ~0.8 | ~3% | — |
| **Total** | **27.36** | 100% | **3.56** |

- 48 audio patches produced; the LLM was called 49× (1 prefill + 48 AR steps), the FM stage 48× (per-patch ODE, 10 steps each).
- RTF = seconds-of-compute per second-of-audio. Whole pipeline is 3.56× realtime; the **LLM layer alone is 0.74× realtime** (already faster than realtime even eager).

## 3.3 Cross-engine -> end-to-end projection

Replacing the eager LLM stage (5.69 s / 20.8%) with a serving engine (~0.1–0.2 s for the same work):

```
e2e_original            = 27.36 s
e2e_with_LLM_engine     ≈ (27.36 − 5.69) + 0.15 ≈ 21.8 s   (−20%)
```

The dominant 18.56 s diffusion stage is untouched by either LLM engine.
